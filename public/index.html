Rules

  • Minimum age 4. Children under 11 must stay beside an adult for the whole distance.
  • Under-18s must be registered by a parent or guardian.
  • Buggies and prams are welcome. Please start at the back.
  • No dogs, bicycles, scooters, skates or any wheels other than buggies and wheelchairs.
  • Wheelchair users are welcome on the paved promenade.
  • This is not a race. There is no timing and no finish order.
  • Please keep to the right and let faster runners pass on the left. The promenade stays open to the public.

Getting there

  • On foot along the promenade — from either direction.
  • By car — paid parking along the seafront and on the side streets; on a Sunday morning there is usually space.
  • City buses stop on the seafront.
  • There are public toilets in the park.
  • Water at the start and the finish. Bring your own bottle — we do not hand out single-use plastic.

No single-use plastic

No single-use plastic. Bring your own bottle — we will refill it at the start and the finish. We take everything we bring away with us, and we would rather leave the seafront cleaner than we found it. If you see litter on the route, pick it up.

Why we run

Most playgrounds have a step, a gate, or sand at the entrance. That is enough to keep a child in a wheelchair on the outside, watching.

We are raising money for a sports playground in Limassol built so that nobody is left on the outside. The organisation that will receive the money is being confirmed now.

Every euro raised will be published here, with the name of the organisation that receives it.

How you will hear the date

When the date is agreed we will email everyone on the list. We will also email you if the run has to be cancelled for weather or any other reason, by 20:00 the evening before at the latest. Email is our only notification channel, so please use an address you check.

Questions

When is the run?

We do not have a date yet. We are agreeing it with the Municipality, and we will email everyone on the list as soon as it is set.

Why is there no date?

We would rather announce a date we can keep than one we have to move. The seafront is shared with other events, and the route needs the Municipality's agreement.

Is the run really free?

Yes. No entry fee, no timing, no pressure. Join the list so we know how many people to expect.

Can children take part?

Yes, with a parent or guardian running or walking with them. The 1 km loop is made for families.

Can I bring my dog?

No. Dogs are not allowed at this run.

Is it accessible?

The seafront path is flat and paved, with no cars. Wheelchair users and buggies are welcome on the 1 km loop.

What do I need to bring?

Water and something to keep the sun off. There is water at the start and finish.

Do I need a medical certificate?

No. You confirm you are fit and healthy when you sign up. If you have any doubt, see a doctor first.

Will there be photos?

Yes, for our website and social media. You can opt out when you sign up, and we will give you a coloured wristband at the start. Email us and we will remove any photo.

How will I hear the date, or that the run is off?

By email, to everyone on the list. If the weather or anything else makes the run unsafe we will cancel it and email you by 20:00 the evening before at the latest.

Where does the money go?

Nothing is collected at the community run. From the 2027 race, every donation goes to the playground project and we publish the total and the recipient.

When can I enter the 2027 race?

Registration opens in spring, before the race. Join the list for the community run and you will hear first.
