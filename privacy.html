Privacy policy

Who we are. Limassol Charity Run is organised by Artem Bobrov in Limassol, Cyprus. For the purposes of the GDPR, Artem Bobrov is the data controller. You can reach us at info@limassolcharityrun.com.

What we collect. When you sign up for the run we collect: your first and last name, your email address, your phone number if you give it, your chosen distance, the name and phone number of an emergency contact, how you heard about us, your language, and the choices you make about photography and future emails. We record the date and time you accepted our Terms of Participation.

Why we collect it, and on what legal basis.

  • To tell you the date of the run, to organise it and to contact you about it, including telling you if it is cancelled. Legal basis: taking steps at your request before entering into an agreement (Article 6(1)(b)).
  • To contact someone on your behalf in a medical emergency. We use your emergency contact only for this. Legal basis: protecting someone's vital interests (Article 6(1)(d)).
  • To take and publish photographs of the event for our website and social media, and to show to potential partners. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in recording the event and building this run (Article 6(1)(f)). You can opt out when you sign up, and you can ask us to remove any photograph at any time. Where you are the main subject of a photograph and we want to give it to a partner for their own use, we will ask you first.
  • To send you news about the 2027 charity race, if and only if you tick that box. Legal basis: your consent (Article 6(1)(a)). You can withdraw it at any time by replying to any email.

Which fields are required. Your name, email and acceptance of the Terms are required — without them we cannot add you to the list. Your phone number, emergency contact, chosen distance, how you heard about us and both consent boxes are optional, and leaving them blank changes nothing about your place in the run. We will ask for an emergency contact again once the date is set.

Who sees your data. Only the organiser. We store registrations in a database hosted by Supabase, and we send email through Google Workspace. Both act as our processors. We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with sponsors.

Where your data goes. Our database and email are hosted on infrastructure that may process data outside the European Economic Area. Where that happens, the transfer is covered by the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

How long we keep it. We delete registration data twelve months after the event. If you asked for news about 2027, we keep your email address until you tell us to stop. Photographs we keep indefinitely as a record of the event, unless you ask us to remove one.

Your rights. You can ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, to delete it, to restrict how we use it, to object to our use of it, or to receive it in a portable form. Write to info@limassolcharityrun.com and we will respond within one month, free of charge.

Complaints. If you think we have handled your data badly, please tell us first — we would rather fix it. You also have the right to complain to the Office of the Commissioner for Personal Data Protection of the Republic of Cyprus.

Cookies. This site sets no tracking cookies, runs no analytics, and loads no advertising or social media trackers. We store your language choice in your browser so the site remembers it; that never leaves your device and is not shared with anyone.

Changes. If we change this policy we will update the date below.
