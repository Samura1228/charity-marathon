Terms of participation

1. The event. The Limassol Charity Run community run takes place at Molos Sculpture Park on the Limassol seafront. The date has not been set yet and will be announced by email to everyone on the list. We plan to meet at 08:00 and start at 08:30. The route is about 5 km out and back on the paved promenade, with a 1 km loop for families. There is no entry fee, no timing and no finish order. This is not a race.

2. Your fitness is your responsibility. By signing up you confirm that you are sufficiently fit and healthy to take part, and that you have no medical condition that makes taking part unsafe. If you are in any doubt, speak to a doctor before you run. We do not ask for a medical certificate and we do not check anyone's health. Do not take part if you feel unwell on the day.

3. You take part at your own risk. You take part entirely at your own risk. The organiser accepts no liability for any loss, damage or expense of any kind arising in connection with your participation, including loss of or damage to property and claims by third parties.

This exclusion does not apply to death or personal injury caused by the organiser's negligence, or to any other liability that cannot be excluded by law.

4. Age and children. The minimum age is 4. Children under 11 must remain beside a responsible adult for the whole distance — the volunteers cannot supervise children. Anyone under 18 must be registered by a parent or guardian, who accepts these terms on their behalf and remains responsible for them throughout.

5. On the route. The promenade stays open to the public. Keep to the right, pass on the left, and give way to other people using it. Follow the instructions of volunteers. Buggies and prams are welcome and should start at the back. Wheelchairs are welcome. Dogs, bicycles, scooters, skates and other wheels are not allowed.

6. Photography. We will photograph the run for our website, our social media and to show potential partners. If you prefer not to be the main subject of a published photograph, tick the box when you sign up and we will give you a coloured wristband at the start. You can ask us to remove any photograph at any time by writing to info@limassolcharityrun.com, and we will do it without asking why. Our privacy policy explains the legal basis.

7. Your place is yours. Places are free and limited to 150. Please do not pass your place to someone else — ask them to sign up themselves, and tell us if you can no longer come so we can free the place.

8. Date and cancellation. We will email the date to everyone on the list once it is agreed. If the weather or anything else then makes the run unsafe, we will cancel it and email everyone, by 20:00 the evening before at the latest. Email is our only notification channel.

9. Your data. How we handle your personal data is set out in our privacy policy.

10. Law. These terms are governed by the law of the Republic of Cyprus.
